# Wedding Peak — 2-Week Look-Your-Best Plan

**Written 2026-09-28 · target: your wedding day, ~2 weeks out · starting point: in shape with a soft layer to shed.**

This is the nutrition/lifestyle half of the plan. The training half lives in the app as the
**Wedding Peak** block (Program tab → *Start 2-week Wedding Peak*). Over two weeks, **nutrition, water,
sodium, sleep and alcohol move the needle more than training does** — so this doc matters at least as much
as the workouts.

---

## The honest goal

You can't add real muscle in 2 weeks. What actually changes how you look on the day:

1. **Drop a little body fat** (a moderate deficit over 14 days = ~1.5–3 lb of real fat, visibly sharper face/waist).
2. **Keep muscles FULL** — glycogen + a good pump make you look bigger and harder, not flat.
3. **Cut the bloat / water** you're carrying from sodium, fibre, and training inflammation.
4. **Peak on the day** — a light pump, good sleep, minimal alcohol/salt the night before.

So the target is: **lean out a touch, stay full, de-bloat, and land it on the day.** Not a crash diet — crashing
flattens you and shows in your face.

---

## How the two weeks are structured

The app runs your 5 sessions/week. Bias is 4 upper/pump days + 1 leg day (legs show less in a suit and drive
more bloat/fatigue). You're doing your own cardio on top.

- **Week 1 — "Sharpen":** full peak volume, keep your working weights, push the pumps. Moderate deficit.
- **Week 2 — "Taper/Peak":** the app auto-drops to **half the sets at the same weights** (it shows as
  "DELOAD WEEK" — that's intentional, it's your taper). This sheds fatigue, inflammation and water so you look
  drier and fuller by the weekend while staying strong.

## Nutrition

**Calories — moderate deficit (~300–500 below maintenance).** Enough to lean out, not so much you go flat or
lose fullness. Don't go lower — aggressive cuts this close cost you muscle fullness and show in your face.

**Protein — the non-negotiable. ~1 g per lb bodyweight per day** (e.g. 180 lb → ~180 g). This is what protects
muscle in a deficit. Spread over 3–4 meals. ([RP — muscle retention in aggressive cuts](https://rpstrength.com/blogs/podcasts/stop-fearing-aggressive-cuts-the-science-of-faster-fat-loss))

**Carbs — keep them, don't fear them.** Carbs fill the muscle (glycogen) and keep you looking full and pumped.
Put most around training. Cutting carbs to zero makes you look *smaller and flatter*, not leaner. Prioritise
"clean" carbs (rice, potato, oats, fruit) over bready/salty/high-fibre-bomb stuff that bloats.

**Fats — moderate**, fill the rest of calories after protein and carbs are set (~0.3–0.4 g/lb). Don't drop fat
too low for too long.

**Rough day (adjust to your bodyweight & maintenance):**
- Protein: ~1 g/lb
- Carbs: ~1–1.5 g/lb (higher on training days, lower on rest)
- Fats: ~0.3–0.4 g/lb
- Veg + water to fill out meals

**Food choices that keep you tight (not bloated):**
- Lean proteins, rice/potato over bread, fruit, minimal processed/packaged food.
- Go easy on: excess dairy, very high-fibre volume, sugar alcohols/"diet" junk, carbonated drinks, and salty
  restaurant/takeout food — all common bloat culprits.
- Drink a lot of water consistently (see below). Being well-hydrated makes you *hold less* water, not more.

---

## Water & sodium — the gentle version (don't do the bodybuilder cut)

Competitors do aggressive water-cutting and sodium-loading/depletion the last few days. **Skip it.** The
evidence is weak, it's easy to get wrong, and for a wedding the downside (looking flat, spilling over, feeling
awful, cramping, a wrecked day) massively outweighs any edge.
([Peak-week carb review, Sports Med Open 2024](https://sportsmedicine-open.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s40798-024-00674-z) ·
[Evidence-based peak week, BMC 2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13102-021-00296-y))

Instead:
- **Drink consistently high water all two weeks** (~3–4 L/day). Steady hydration = less water retention.
- **Keep sodium steady and moderate** — just avoid *spikes*. The bloated look the morning after a salty
  restaurant meal is exactly what you're avoiding, especially the last 3 days.
- **Don't** slam water then cut it, and **don't** salt-load/deplete. Boring and steady wins.

---

## The last 3 days (Thu–Sat for a Saturday wedding)

- Keep protein high, keep training light (week-2 taper handles this).
- **Dial back bloat triggers:** lower fibre volume, avoid salty/greasy meals out, skip carbonated drinks and
  excess dairy, no new/unfamiliar foods (no GI surprises on the day).
- **Keep carbs normal-to-slightly-higher** so muscles stay full — do NOT deplete.
- Hydrate well through Friday; on Saturday drink to thirst (don't force litres right before).
- **Alcohol:** minimise all week; ideally little to none Thu/Fri. Alcohol bloats the face, wrecks sleep, and
  flattens you. Save it for the reception.
- **Sleep 7–9 h**, especially Thu/Fri — sleep visibly affects face puffiness and how you carry water.

---

## Wedding morning — the pump

- Normal breakfast with protein + some clean carbs (oats, fruit, eggs). Nothing salty/greasy/novel.
- **Optional 10–15 min pump before you get dressed:** light bands or bodyweight — push-ups, a few sets of
  lateral raises / curls / band pull-aparts to failure-ish, some ab work. Just enough to bring blood into the
  shoulders/arms/chest. Don't fatigue yourself or get sweaty — the goal is a light, full look.
- Water to thirst. You'll look your fullest and sharpest a couple hours after eating + a light pump.

---

## Cardio (you're running your own)

- Great lever for the deficit. **Zone 2 / incline walking (30–45 min most days)** burns fat without adding
  much fatigue or appetite — ideal in a peak.
- Keep hard intervals to 1–2x/week max so you don't add leg fatigue/water or eat into recovery.
- Steps: aim high daily (10k+). Easy, low-cost fat loss.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Crash diet / very low calories — flat, drawn face, lost fullness.
- ❌ Zero carbs — smaller and flatter, not leaner.
- ❌ Water cut + sodium manipulation — high risk, low reward for a non-competitor.
- ❌ New supplements, "detox," laxatives, or anything you haven't used before.
- ❌ A big salty/boozy meal the night before.
